How a part gets made

Coil to finished component

A straight, traceable sequence — each step is where it is because the one before it has to happen first.

01
Coil feed & blanking
Steel coil is uncoiled and fed into the press; blanks are cut to the part's flat pattern.
02
Forming / single-hit stamping
50–300 ton presses form brackets, structural panels, and mounting components to spec.
03
Inspection & export packing
Dimensional check against drawing tolerances, then export-grade packing out of Busan port.
